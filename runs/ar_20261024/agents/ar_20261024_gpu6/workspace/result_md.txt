# exp_cap_head_dim_64: HEAD_DIM 128 -> 64 (n_head 4 -> 8) — DISCARD

## Experiment
One change vs champion `exp_tput_batch_2e17` (val_bpb 1.088286):

```python
HEAD_DIM = 64   # was 128
```

At DEPTH 8 / ASPECT_RATIO 64 this keeps `model_dim = ((512 + 63)//64)*64 = 512` and moves
`n_head` 4 -> 8, `head_dim` 128 -> 64. Attention FLOPs are unchanged (`h*q = 512` either way);
params change only in `ve_gate` (`nn.Linear(32, n_kv_head)`, 4 VE layers): +512, exactly as
predicted in gpu3's [GPU-REVIEW] on the proposal. This is a **step-neutral** representational
probe — it isolates head granularity from step count.

## Result
- **val_bpb = 1.096009** vs champion 1.088286 -> **delta +0.007723 (worse)**
- |delta| = 2.70 sigma (sigma = 0.002865, 2-sigma = 0.00573) -> **outside the
  noise band**: a real, if modest, regression. No second seed needed.
- Outcome: **DISCARD**. Champion unchanged at 1.088286.
- Steps 948 vs 956 (-0.8%): the "zero-FLOP" prediction held — the change is effectively step-neutral.
- tok/s 408.5K vs 411.3K (-0.7%): flash-attn3 at head_dim 64 is very slightly slower, as gpu3's
  caveat warned; there is no throughput win to offset the quality loss.
- Params 41.9M vs 41.94M (+512 ve_gate scalars, as predicted). Peak VRAM 19,176 MB.
- Total 483.6s (training 300.1s). Startup+eval was ~100s longer than the champion run because
  max-autotune re-benchmarks the new 8-head attention bmm shapes; steady-state tok/s is unaffected.

## Interpretation (capacity hypothesis lens)
At a fixed step count (948 vs 956), halving head_dim and doubling head count costs +0.0077 bpb.
Two confounded mechanisms, both plausibly harmful at this width:
1. **Rotary granularity:** `_precompute_rotary_embeddings` halves the rotary channels 64 -> 32
   (lowest inv_freq 1.1e-4 -> 1.4e-4; longest wavelength still > 2048, so this is granularity, not
   a loss of long-range reach).
2. **Smaller per-head subspace:** 8 heads of 64 vs 4 heads of 128 at width 512.

The capacity team's hypothesis (more capacity/structure helps) is **not** supported here in the
"more heads" direction — but this is a representational-granularity result, not a capacity result
(params moved by 0.001%). Practical read: **keep HEAD_DIM=128**. The axis now has n=1 in the
decrease direction; the increase direction (head_dim 256, n_head 2) stays cold and is a priori risky.
